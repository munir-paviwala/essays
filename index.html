Let's unpack why I said that:

It takes a lot of 'freedom' to do art. Freedom To waste resources.

Very simply, some bahut purani (very old) societies were formed in order to get some freedom but kabhi toh (sometimes) you just couldn't. In cases of pregnancy, injury, caretaking kids, old age, etc. some members of the community had to take time off from producing/foraging resources.

Producing/foraging resources was a 'productive' task. A productive task that helped to survive.

These resources - food, fuel, shelter and clothing - were necessary to live. Gathering these takes energy. To collect, humans had to spend energy that came from food itself, fire made it easier to access that food energy. Beyond immediate needs, shelter and clothing helped survive longer - but took energy to make.

All this energy gives freedom to not die from hunger or environment. Some may call this earliest phase of 'history', atleast John Richard Hicks of A theory of Economic History (1969) would say this.[^1] A lot of this also happens to be the perspective that is prevelant in the energy history. The mapping of energy flows matters.
